# Business problem

New Hampshire housing decisions require data spread across federal demographic surveys, state property-tax publications, and federal housing-market indexes. Differences in geography, reporting periods, formats, and definitions make cross-source analysis costly and error-prone.

The platform will provide a governed analytical layer for comparing housing affordability, local property-tax burden, assessed value, and house-price movement. Primary users are analysts and decision-makers who need consistent statewide and municipal trend reporting.

## Initial questions

- How do income and housing costs vary across New Hampshire communities?
- How have assessed values and municipal tax rates changed over time?
- How do regional house-price trends compare with demographic and affordability measures?
- Where do source coverage, grain, or methodology limit comparison?
