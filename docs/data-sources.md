# Data sources

## U.S. Census American Community Survey

Planned measures include population, households, household income, tenure, vacancy, rent, home value, and housing costs. Dataset vintage, survey product, geography, variable identifiers, and margins of error must be retained.

## New Hampshire Department of Revenue Administration

Planned inputs include published municipal tax rates, equalized valuations, and related property-tax data. Ingestion must preserve the publication year and original file provenance because layouts and definitions may change.

## Federal Housing Finance Agency

Planned inputs include House Price Index series suitable for New Hampshire and available sub-state geographies. Models must retain index type, frequency, geography, and revision metadata.

Exact datasets, endpoints, licenses, refresh cadences, and data contracts will be recorded before implementation.
